Method and limitations

MNDWI = (green − SWIR) / (green + SWIR), bands B03 and B11, on a shared 20 m grid with reflectance offset correction. SCL masks cloud, shadow, snow and invalid pixels. New water must cross both the water and the change threshold and must not already have been water before.

Turbid water, wet soil, roofs, vegetation and mixed pixels can cause errors. An industrial area is not a confirmed company asset. OSM nodes are drawn with an explicitly approximate 60 m radius.

Validation uses the same sensor and water index as its own target, not an independent measurement. One event only. The no-new-flooding baseline is a weak reference; a strong weather/gauge comparison is still missing. The duration extrapolation fits one exponential rate from a single observed interval -- real drainage (pumping, dams, further rain) will not actually follow a constant rate, so treat it as illustrative, not predictive. Thresholds changed after revealing the outcome are exploratory, not an untouched test.

A wet surrounding area proves neither closed roads nor the absence of alternative routes. No invented supply chains. The spatial severity index is not validated as a trading strategy. The news/weather timeline is a fixed historical record, not a live feed, and does not reconcile independently reported numbers that differ slightly across outlets.
